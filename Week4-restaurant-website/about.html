A LITTLE ABOUT US

Inspired by home.
Open to the world.

Saffron & Stone began with a simple thought: the best meals are the ones that make you feel welcome. We wanted a neighbourhood table where traditional Indian flavours meet fresh ideas, without losing the comfort that makes food special.

Our kitchen celebrates seasonal produce, thoughtful sourcing and recipes made with patience. Whether you're here for lunch, a family celebration or dinner with friends, there's always room at our table.
